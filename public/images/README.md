# Images

Drop photographs here using these exact filenames. Until a file exists, the
site renders a dark gradient placeholder in its place — nothing breaks.

| Filename                        | Where it appears              | Suggested size |
|---------------------------------|-------------------------------|----------------|
| `hero.jpg`                      | Homepage hero                 | 2400 × 1400    |
| `office.jpg`                     | Homepage intro, About page    | 1200 × 1500    |
| `approach.jpg`                   | Homepage "Our approach"       | 1200 × 1500    |
| `practice-<slug>.jpg`            | Practice area cards & pages   | 1200 × 800     |
| `attorney-<slug>.jpg`            | Attorney cards & profiles     | 900 × 1200     |

Slugs come from `src/content/firm.ts`. With the current content that means
`practice-corporate.jpg`, `practice-disputes.jpg`, `practice-property.jpg`,
`practice-employment.jpg`.

Compress before committing — aim for under 300 KB each. Squoosh.app works well.

**Licensing:** only use photographs the firm owns or has licensed. Stock
photos of models are common on law firm sites but must not be presented as
the firm's own attorneys or offices.
